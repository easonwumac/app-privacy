更新日期：2026-09-30

本機資料與備份

影片、行程、常用目的地與設定保存在裝置。行程軌跡預設關閉；開啟後保存接受的定位點。影片、行程及目的地可能依 iOS 設定包含於系統備份。離線測速資料包及自願啟用的實測紀錄排除系統備份。

實測紀錄包含精確位置、規劃路線、車速、相對位移與里程辨識結果，不包含影像、聲音、藍牙裝置識別或原始裝置開機時間。分享前請確認位置資訊；匯出由使用者選擇接收者。

相機、位置及語音

相機僅在前景提供實景及無聲錄影；背景停止相機，導航仍可使用位置。麥克風由語音按鈕啟用，只使用裝置端辨識，不支援時可輸入文字。Apple Foundation Models 在支援裝置上本機處理指令，不可用時使用基本規則。BLE OBD 由使用者選擇連接，車速用於粗略隧道估算。

外部連線

Apple MapKit 提供地圖、搜尋、路線及路況圖層；相關搜尋文字、起點及目的地會傳至 Apple。公開資料更新連線至資料來源；OSM 附近下載將選擇的地理範圍送至 Overpass。地理 AR 會依目前位置與地理錨點，連線向 Apple 取得定位參考影像及地理資料，並使用相機畫面協助視覺定位。Overpass 以網路位址管理流量，可能保存包含區域座標的查詢紀錄；區域座標可反推出查詢中心，可能近似你的所在位置。外部服務依其自身政策處理請求。Lumiro 沒有帳號、廣告、追蹤 SDK 或自有上傳伺服器。

刪除與支援

可在片段、行程、常用目的地、離線資料及實測頁面刪除對應本機資料。權限可在 iOS 設定變更。解除安裝會移除 App 本機資料，但不會清除已分享檔案或既有系統備份。

English

Clips, trips, saved places and settings stay on the device and may be included in iOS backups. Route trace recording is off by default. Offline camera packs and optional diagnostic logs are excluded from backups. Logs may contain precise locations, routes, speeds and relative sensor measurements, but no camera images, audio, Bluetooth identifiers or raw device uptime.

Camera capture is foreground only and silent; navigation may use location in the background. Voice recognition and Foundation Models processing are on device when supported, with typed input/basic-command fallbacks. MapKit requests go to Apple; public-data downloads contact their sources, and Overpass receives the selected geographic bounds. Geographic AR uses location and anchor-coordinate requests to obtain Apple's localization imagery and geographic data, and camera captures assist visual localization. Overpass uses network addresses to manage traffic and may retain queries containing geographic bounds; their center can approximate your location. Those services have their own policies. Lumiro has no accounts, ads, tracking SDK or app-owned upload server.

Delete local data from its respective screen and change permissions in iOS Settings. You choose whether and where to export/share. Shared copies and existing system backups are not removed by deleting app data. Contact easonwumac@gmail.com.
